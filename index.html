06
New risks / New controls

Mitigating risk

The biggest risks remain the same whether a ticket is triaged automatically or manually. New risks have specific mitigations in place

Guardrail

Auto-route only with high confidence

  • Launch scope is limited to categories with sufficient training data and demonstrated classification confidence
  • Receiving teams can follow current Departmental Triage best practices to mitigate misrouted tickets
  • Recurring patterns are reported and used to improve the model. Auto-routing is intended to reduce misrouting over time
Guardrail

Only hunt the white whale: Duo 2FA 🐋

  • Send an automatic response only to Duo requests submitted from a York University email address
  • Respond only when a Duo request is missing required information
  • Halo sends approved text based on an AI trigger, not AI-generated free-form advice
  • If the follow-up doesn't apply, a Client Services agent takes over and continues as usual
Guardrail

Dashboard for rapid response

  • Stalled tickets appear on the Otto Excellence dashboard
  • The UIT Service Desk follows a simple recovery process
Guardrail

Better every day

  • During two weeks of silent production monitoring, we compare AI and agent decisions
  • We only go live when monitoring meets the agreed readiness criteria
  • Low-confidence and out-of-scope tickets stay in the human process
  • Every day, we gain more and better data to fine-tune the solution

Impact matrix

Who feels the change?

Requesters

Low impact
What changes
  • Some requests reach the people who can help sooner
  • Incomplete Duo requests may receive an automatic reply requesting missing information
What stays
  • They continue to use the same service channels
  • The same support staff will resolve their requests
Required action
  • Respond to automatic follow-ups when requested

IT agents

Low impact
What changes
  • Some tickets will be automatically routed to the following queues:
    • CTS
    • Classroom Operations
    • ED&I
    • Learning Technology Services
    • UIT Service Desk
    • Telecommunications
  • Tickets may reach all queues faster than before, whether routed automatically or manually
What stays
  • Current review, response, escalation, categorization, and resolution practices remain in place
Required action
  • Monitor ticket queues early and often
  • Follow Halo best practices, especially for handling misrouted tickets and verifying categorization before resolution
  • Notify OpEx about persistent routing errors
    • Raise an Incident to OpEx: Application > HaloITSM > Triage Agent Issue

UIT Service Desk

Medium Impact
What changes
  • Less manual routing for confidently assessed in-scope ticket
  • The Service Desk monitors and recovers stalled tickets
What stays
  • The Service Desk continues to triage low-confidence, out-of-scope, incomplete, and exceptional requests
Required action
  • Monitor the queue and Otto Excellence Dashboard
  • Avoid interrupting active Otto Excellence processing
  • Recover stalled tickets
  • Notify OpEx about persistent routing or stalling errors
    • Raise an Incident to OpEx: Application > HaloITSM > Triage Agent Issue

IT leaders and managers

Low impact
What changes
  • Leaders receive clearer operational evidence about routing performance, exceptions, and readiness
What stays
  • Service ownership, escalation accountability, and human responsibility remain with people
What we need
  • Reinforce expectations
  • Confirm team readiness
  • Support evidence-based tuning during peak
